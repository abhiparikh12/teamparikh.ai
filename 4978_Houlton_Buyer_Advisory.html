Executive Summary

This is a 2006-built, 5-bed/4-bath home in Dublin's Sonata HOA community, inspected April 3, 2026. The overall structure was built in workmanlike fashion and the home shows no major structural defects. However, both the CBI inspection report and the HOA/disclosure review reveal a meaningful list of items requiring attention. The property was unoccupied at time of inspection, which warrants heightened scrutiny.

No Carbon Monoxide Detectors Anywhere in the Home
Safety / Legal Interior
California law requires CO alarms on each level of any home with a fuel-burning appliance or attached garage. This home has a gas furnace, gas cooktop, gas water heater, gas fireplace, and an attached garage — CO detectors are legally mandated and their absence is a required disclosure item at point of sale.
Request seller install CA State Fire Marshal-listed CO detectors on all three levels before close. This is a standard seller obligation in California real estate transactions. Cost is minimal (~$30–60 per unit) but non-negotiable from a legal standpoint.
