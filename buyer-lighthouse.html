Executive Summary

Lighthouse Marina & Resort is a ±32.26-acre RV resort at 151 Brannan Island Road in unincorporated Sacramento County, on the Mokelumne River side of Brannan-Andrus Island. It is offered by CRI Brokerage at $4,500,000, presented as a 7.60% cap rate on $342,005 of adjusted net operating income. The fee parcel (APN 156-0110-070) is vested in 151 Brannan Island Road, LLC. Permit Classification

Domestic Water Supply Permit 3410035
▶ReviewPermit limits year-round residents plus staff to 24
Condition 17 of the permit states the system "shall not exceed more than 24 employees, residences or combination of the two, for greater than 120 days out of the year," and that exceeding this requires contacting the County for reclassification, which "will result in increased chemical and bacteriological monitoring." The September 2026 rent roll lists 66 extended-stay accounts, 60 of them in place longer than nine months.

Under the California Health and Safety Code, a system serving at least 15 service connections used by year-round residents, or at least 25 year-round residents, is a community water system. Community systems must meet the arsenic MCL, which generally does not apply to transient non-community systems.
Consider: request the County EMD water-system file (inspection reports, notices, annual reports and any reclassification correspondence), and have a water engineer scope arsenic and manganese treatment or an alternative source.
▶PlanTreatment cost is not yet scoped
No treatment study is in the data room. As a planning range only, arsenic treatment for a small system (adsorption media or coagulation-filtration) is often in the low-to-mid six figures of capital plus annual media and operating costs. An engineer's estimate should replace this range.
▶PlanWell capacity and the restaurant connection
The seller reports well capacity of about 23 gallons per minute. The 2015 permit lists source capacity as "Unknown" and describes 320 water hook-ups, 13 permanent building hook-ups, an office, a store, a restaurant and two bathroom buildings. The seller states the restaurant's shared use of the well and septic must continue after the sale.
Consider: a capacity test and a written utility agreement with the restaurant operator that allocates cost, compliance responsibility and the right to end service if the regulator requires it.
▶RecordOperator and sampling duties
The permit requires a California Certified Distribution System Operator (Grade 1 or above), monthly coliform sampling with chlorine residuals, quarterly wellhead sampling, annual backflow testing, and an electronic annual report. A cross-connection control plan was prepared in June 2025 by Quality Service Inc.

Statutes to Review With Counsel

▶ReviewRecreational Vehicle Park Occupancy Law — "resident" after 9 months
Civil Code §799.31 defines a resident as a tenant who has occupied a lot for nine months or more. Under §799.70, a resident's tenancy may be ended only on at least 60 days' written notice and for a listed reason (non-payment, rule violations after notice, substantial annoyance, specified convictions, condemnation or change of use). §799.79 allows up to $500 per willful violation. 60 of the 66 accounts have occupied more than nine months.
Consider: treat the current occupants as long-term residents in your underwriting, and confirm with counsel how the 30-day termination clause applies to them.
▶ReviewHCD permit lists the property as a mobilehome park
The HCD Permit to Operate carries Park ID 34-0096-MP and lists 24 mobilehome lots with drains, 120 RV lots with drains and 56 lots without drains (200 total). The Mobilehome Residency Law can extend to recreational vehicles that occupy a mobilehome site for nine or more continuous months (Civil Code §798.3(b)), with its own notice and termination rules. Which law governs which lots is a legal question.
Consider: ask counsel to map each occupied site to its permitted lot type and confirm the governing statute.
▶PlanRent growth depends on turnover
Average rent for 2012–2016 move-ins is about $714, against about $1,076 for 2025–2026 move-ins. Legacy contract caps and resident status mean the gap is likely to close mainly as occupants leave on their own.
▶RecordSecurity deposits
The rent roll shows $40,265 of deposits. Consider requiring a deposit schedule with balances and holding accounts, and a closing credit.

Long-Term Outlook (5–15 Years)

Beyond the purchase, several forces shape what this property is worth at resale.

▶OutlookState Lands lease ends December 31, 2030
Resale value depends on whether the marina, launch and restaurant continue under a new long-term lease and who holds it. Without them, the resort is an RV park behind a levee with documented access rights only.
▶OutlookFlood, levee and insurance costs
The island sits below river level with levees that are not FEMA-accredited. Insurance premiums, levee assessments and lender scrutiny are likely to rise over a long hold.
▶OutlookTenant protections for long-term RV occupants
California has continued to legislate on RV and mobilehome tenancies (for example, AB 1472 in 2024 addressing re-registration to avoid resident status). Long-tenured occupants make repositioning toward short-term guests gradual.
▶OutlookInfrastructure age
Water, septic, electrical distribution and roads date largely to 1979. Plan for a multi-year capital program in addition to the water work.
▶OutlookDemand and operating levers
The property is within roughly an hour of Sacramento and the Bay Area, with a large storage yard, an amenity core and growing extended-stay rents on new agreements. Disciplined pricing, storage marketing and membership terms on the current form are the main levers.
